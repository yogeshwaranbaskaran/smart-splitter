Privacy Policy

Smart Splitter · last updated 21 September 2026

Smart Splitter is a small, non-commercial app for splitting bills with friends. This page explains what data it handles.

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your email address and Google account ID, used only to identify you. We never see your password.
  • Username: the name you choose, shown to people in your groups instead of your email.
  • Bills and splits: the items, amounts, groups and selections you enter or scan.

Bill scanning

When you scan a bill, the image is sent to Google's Gemini API to read the items and prices. The image is used only for that reading and is not stored by Smart Splitter.

Where data is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database. It is used only to run the app. We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or any third party.

Emails

You may receive an email when a split is added to a group you belong to. These are sent via Brevo.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all your data, email the address below and it will be removed.
